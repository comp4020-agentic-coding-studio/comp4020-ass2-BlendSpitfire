The hour that states the course's position, before any of the material has to
argue for it.

Most people arrive at a fighter aircraft through its combat record, and expect
a course about it to be a course about winning. This is not that course. Over
twelve weeks you will learn to take a single-seat jet off the ground, fly a
circuit, and land it. That is the entire syllabus, and it is enough.

The lecture makes the case for that scope in three parts: what the aircraft
actually is, why it cannot be flown without a computer, and why a course that
promises less than you expected is promising the part that is hard.

It also explains the order of the syllabus, which surprises people. The first
practical period teaches egress — how to get out. Not because we expect you to
need it, but because it is the skill with the shortest deadline, and a course
that teaches it in week 11 has quietly decided which weeks matter.

## Reading

The aircraft description chapters of the flight manual, in full. You are not
expected to retain them. You are expected to have seen how much there is.
