**LEDGER** · 2,400 hours in type · Eleven years on test

Wren flew acceptance and envelope-expansion sorties before teaching, which is
a job that consists almost entirely of finding out what an aircraft does at
the edge of what it was cleared for, and then landing it anyway.

She wrote this course's syllabus around the one thing that work taught her: an
aircraft is not a thing you win against. It is a thing you are lent, and the
only outcome the school records is whether it came back able to fly again.

She teaches weeks 1 to 4, runs the week 12 check rides, and signs the
authorisation for your first solo. If she declines to sign it, that is not a
mark against you. It means the gate did its job.
