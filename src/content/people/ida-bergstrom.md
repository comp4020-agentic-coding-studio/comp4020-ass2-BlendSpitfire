Crew chief · Not aircrew · Twenty-two years on the airframe

Ida does not teach a period and does not hold a callsign, and she is the
single most consequential person in this course. The aircraft is hers. You
sign it out from her before a sortie and you sign it back in afterwards, and
between those two signatures you are borrowing it.

She briefs the walkaround in week 1 and she is present at every sortie from
week 10. If she declares an aircraft unserviceable, it does not fly, and there
is no one above her you can appeal that to.

Students remember this course for the first solo. Ida remembers it as the
twelve weeks she lent an airframe to someone who had never flown one.
