## The brief

> Fly the whole profile, in the box, unassisted, to tolerance, in one sortie.

Takeoff, circuit, approach, landing. No instructor input, no resets, no
component flown twice. The tolerances are the published ones and they are on
the standards page; nothing here is a surprise, which is the point of a check.

Final is flown with the airspeed indicator covered. You have been practising
this since week 7.

## What you submit

Nothing written. The check is the sortie, and its result is a signature or the
absence of one.

## Attempts

There is no fixed number. Book the box, fly it again, and again after that.
The instructor would rather sign this in week 9 than sign it early, and a
check passed on the fourth attempt is worth exactly what one passed on the
first is worth.

## What passing it does

It releases the week 10 authorisation for the convenor's signature. Both
signatures are required before the aircraft leaves the line with you in
it; there is no conditional or provisional version of that authorisation.
