## Before you arrive

Nothing. This is the one period of the course with no preparation, and that is
deliberate. Everything you learn today is what you would need if the course
went wrong on its first day.

## The period

You will sit in the seat on the ground, in the hangar, with the aircraft
switched off, and you will practise leaving it until you can do it without
looking down.

The seat will fire at zero altitude and zero airspeed. It will also fire
outside that envelope, in a descending turn, at low level, with results that
depend on numbers you are about to memorise. The decision to use it is
irreversible and has to be made faster than you can reason, which means it has
to be made before you need it — in the brief, on the ground, with a figure in
feet attached to it.

In the second half Ida walks you around the airframe. You will look at
intakes, control surfaces, tyres and panels, and you will not sign the release
form, because you are not yet anyone who can.

## Afterwards

Write down your personal minimum ejection altitude and bring it to week 2.
You will be asked to justify it, and you will revise it at least twice before
week 10.
