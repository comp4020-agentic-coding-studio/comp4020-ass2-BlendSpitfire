## Before you arrive

Nothing. This is the one period of the course with no preparation, and that
is deliberate: everything you learn today is what you would need if the
course went wrong on its first day.

## In this period

You sit in the seat on the ground, in the hangar, with the aircraft switched
off, and practise leaving it until you can do it without looking down.

The seat fires under two conditions:

- at zero altitude and zero airspeed
- outside that envelope, in a descending turn, at low level, with results
  that depend on numbers you are about to memorise

The decision to use it is irreversible and has to be made faster than you can
reason it through, so it is made in advance — in the brief, on the ground, as
a specific altitude in feet.

In the second half, Ida walks you around the airframe: intakes, control
surfaces, tyres, panels. You do not sign the release form in this period,
because you are not yet qualified to.

## Afterwards

Write down your personal minimum ejection altitude and bring it to week 2.
You will be asked to justify it, and you will revise it at least twice before
week 10.
