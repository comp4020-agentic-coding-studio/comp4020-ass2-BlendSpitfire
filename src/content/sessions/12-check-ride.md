## Before you arrive

Your card, your gates, and your decision altitude in the form you defended in
week 11.

## In this period

One takeoff, one circuit, one landing, flown to the tolerances published on
the standards page. Wren observes from the tower and Ida receives the
aircraft.

Two things about how this is graded, stated here rather than left to be
discovered:

- a go-around is not a failure. If your gates say to abandon the approach and
  you abandon it, you have demonstrated the thing this course is for.
- flying a marginal approach to a landing because it is the check ride is the
  failure the examiner is watching for.

The mark is not an average of your control inputs. It is a judgement on the
sortie as a whole, answering the question the course has asked since week 1:
can this aircraft fly again tomorrow, and was that the outcome of what you
did rather than of what you got away with.

## Afterwards

Sign it back in. The course ends when the airframe is serviceable and in
Ida's hands.
