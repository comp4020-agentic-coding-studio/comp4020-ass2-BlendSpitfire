## Before you arrive

Your card, your gates, and your decision altitude in the form you defended in
week 11.

## The period

One takeoff, one circuit, one landing, flown to the tolerances published on
the standards page. Wren observes from the tower and Ida receives the
aircraft.

Two things about how this is graded, stated here rather than left to be
discovered:

A go-around is not a failure. If your gates say to abandon the approach and
you abandon it, you have demonstrated the thing this course is for. Flying a
marginal approach to a landing because it is the check ride is the failure,
and it is the one the examiner is actually watching for.

The mark is not an average of your control inputs. It is a judgement on the
sortie as a whole, and the question it answers is the one the course has asked
since week 1: can this aircraft fly again tomorrow, and was that the outcome
of what you did rather than of what you got away with.

## Afterwards

Sign it back in. The course is over at the point where the airframe is
serviceable and in Ida's hands, which is where it was in week 1, which is the
only place it was ever supposed to end up.
