## Before you arrive

Your two sets of refusal speeds from week 5, and the difference between them
in feet of runway.

## In this period

The stick on your right does not travel. It moves about a quarter of an inch
at full deflection; the earliest aircraft of this type had a stick that did
not move at all, and a small amount of give was later added to it.

You are not moving a surface. You are applying a pressure that a computer
reads as a request, and the computer's response arrives faster than your
proprioception can confirm it. The characteristic failure of this period
follows from that: you correct, the aircraft has already corrected, you feel
your own correction arrive as an error, and you correct that.

That is a pilot-induced oscillation: the aircraft is not oscillating, the
pilot is — the system is executing a series of requests, each a response to
the previous one already being answered.

The fix is to release the stick rather than correct more gently, and let the
aircraft settle. It does so in under a second.

## Afterwards

Nothing written. Sit in the box during an unbooked slot and hold straight and
level for five minutes without correcting anything. Most students cannot do
this on the first attempt.
