## Before you arrive

Your six covered-indicator circuits from week 7, with the resulting
airspeeds.

## In this period

Touchdown is not the end of the approach — it is the start of a deceleration
flown with the same wing, on the ground.

Hold the nose up after the main wheels touch and the aircraft becomes a large
airbrake: the wing that was lifting is now mostly drag, which slows the
aircraft more effectively than the wheel brakes at that speed. There is a
speed below which the elevator can no longer hold the nose up; lower it
deliberately just before that point, rather than waiting for it to fall on
its own.

The second half of the period is the progression check:

- flown to the published tolerances
- not negotiable
- with no fixed number of attempts

Tomas signs it in week 9 rather than sign it early.

## Afterwards

If you passed, the authorisation for week 10 goes to Wren for signature. If
you did not, book the box — nobody flies the aircraft without this signature.
