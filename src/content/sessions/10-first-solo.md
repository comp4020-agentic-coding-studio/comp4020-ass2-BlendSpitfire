## Before you arrive

The signed authorisation, your written gates, and eight hours of sleep that
the schedule has made room for and will check.

## The period

One takeoff. One circuit. One landing.

There is no instructor seat in this aircraft. That is not a decision the
school made; it is what the airframe is, and it means the first time you fly
this type is the first time you fly it alone. Every period since week 1 exists
because of that fact.

Wren is on the radio and Ida is on the line. Neither of them can help you, and
both of them will be there anyway.

You will be given a gate for the sortie itself, of the same form as the ones
you wrote in week 9: conditions under which you do not start, and conditions
under which you return without completing the circuit. Using it is not a
failure. It is the thing the previous nine weeks were teaching.

## Afterwards

Sign the aircraft back in with Ida. Then write, the same day, before you
discuss it with anyone, the point in the sortie at which you were furthest
behind the aircraft, and what you did about it. This becomes the first page of
your week 11 submission.
