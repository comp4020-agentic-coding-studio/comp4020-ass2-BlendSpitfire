## Before you arrive

The signed authorisation, your written gates, and eight hours of sleep that
the schedule has made room for and will check.

## In this period

One takeoff. One circuit. One landing.

There is no instructor seat in this aircraft. That is not a choice the school
made; it is a property of the airframe, and it means the first flight in this
type is a solo flight. Every period since week 1 exists because of that fact.

Wren is on the radio and Ida is on the line. Neither can assist directly.

You fly under a gate for the sortie itself, in the same form as the ones
written in week 9: conditions under which you do not start, and conditions
under which you return without completing the circuit. Using it is not a
failure — it is what the previous nine weeks were for.

## Afterwards

Sign the aircraft back in with Ida. Then, the same day and before discussing
it with anyone, write the point in the sortie at which you were furthest
behind the aircraft, and what you did about it. This becomes the first page
of your week 11 submission.
