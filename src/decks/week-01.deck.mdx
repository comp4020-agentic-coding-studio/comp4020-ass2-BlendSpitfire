# What this course is for

SLOP4386 · Week 1

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# This course does not teach air combat

---

## It teaches three things

- take off
- fly one circuit
- land

Twelve weeks. That is the whole syllabus.

---

## Why that is enough

The hard part was never the shooting.

The hard part is that this aircraft is difficult to fly and expensive to
break, and there is nobody in it but you.

---

## The aircraft

Its centre of gravity sits behind its aerodynamic centre.

Disturb it in pitch and the disturbance **grows**.

---

## That was on purpose

An aircraft that resists being pointed somewhere is slow to point.

Remove the resistance and it points immediately.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# You cannot fly it

---

## Nobody can

The corrections arrive faster than a human produces them.

So a human does not make them.

---

## Four computers do

They sample your stick, sample the aircraft, vote, and move the surfaces
many times a second.

Your hand does not reach the control surfaces.

---

## So what is your job

You state what the aircraft should be doing.

Something else decides how.

Twelve weeks on what that leaves you responsible for.

---

## The order of the syllabus

Week 1 is not the cockpit tour.

Week 1 is how to get out.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# The skill with the shortest deadline

---

## Week 10

This type has no second seat.

Your first flight in it is solo.

Everything before week 10 exists because of that sentence.

---

## The gate

No simulator check, no authorisation.

No authorisation, no aircraft.

This does not bend.

---

## What you are graded on

Not elegance. Not the landing.

---

{/* _class: centered */}

## One question

Can this aircraft fly again tomorrow —

and was that what you did, or what you got away with?
