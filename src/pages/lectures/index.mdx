Six of the twelve weeks carry a lecture. The simulator weeks and the flying
weeks do not — there's nothing to lecture on once the work has moved to the
box or the aircraft.

The lecture makes the argument and sets the reading; the period applies it.
Week 11 is written as the answer to the question week 3 leaves open, and the
week 1 deck is the one you can read without being in the room.
