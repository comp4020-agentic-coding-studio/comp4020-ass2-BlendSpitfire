Two of these are gates rather than grades. The systems examination in week 3
has a timed practical component, and the simulator check in week 8 releases
the authorisation for your first solo — without it the aircraft does not leave
the line with you in it.

The check ride is half the mark and is judged as one sortie rather than as a
sum of its parts. Abandoning an approach on your own gates costs you nothing
there. Continuing past one is the failure, and it stays a failure when the
landing turns out fine.
