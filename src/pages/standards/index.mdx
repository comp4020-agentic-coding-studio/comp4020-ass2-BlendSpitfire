Every number this course marks you against is here. Nothing on a check ride is
held to a figure that is not on this page, and nothing here changes during the
semester.

Know them. Not as a reference you can look up — you will be at 300 knots when
you need most of them.

## Circuit profile

| Point | Height | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upwind, climbing | to 1500 ft | 300 kt |
| Downwind | 1500 ft | 250 kt |
| Base turn | 1500 ft descending | 200 kt |
| Final, configured | on profile | on AOA |

Speeds are targets until the base turn. After it there is no target speed,
because after it you are flying angle of attack.

## Approach reference

| Reference | Value |
| --- | --- |
| On-speed angle of attack | 13° |
| Indexer indication | centre light only |
| Airspeed on final | whatever results |

The last row is not a joke. The speed that keeps the wing flying changes with
weight and fuel state; the angle at which it stops flying does not. Fly the
angle and accept the speed.

## Tolerances

Applied to the week 8 progression check and the week 12 check ride, and to
nothing else.

| Item | Tolerance |
| --- | --- |
| Circuit height | ± 100 ft |
| Downwind speed | ± 10 kt |
| Angle of attack on final | ± 1 unit |
| Centreline at touchdown | ± 10 ft |
| Touchdown zone | first 1000 ft past threshold |

## Enforced limits

The aircraft will not exceed these. You do not need to remember them under
load, and they are listed so you can tell them apart from the next section.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Symmetric G | +9.0 / −3.0 |
| Angle of attack | 25° |
| Roll rate | system limited |

## Restrictions

The aircraft will do all of these. Unlike the limits above, the system will
not stop you — holding to these under load is on you.

- No flight below 500 ft AGL outside the published pattern
- No approach continued past a gate that is not met
- No sortie started inside twelve hours of crew rest
- No recovery attempted below your stated decision altitude

Week 3 asks you to tell this section from the one above it. This page is the
answer key, and the distinction is the most consequential thing on it.

## Weather minima

| Sortie | Ceiling | Vis | Xwind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Simulator | — | — | — |
| First solo | 2500 ft | 8 km | 10 kt |
| Check ride | 1500 ft | 5 km | 15 kt |

Minima are floors, not targets. A sortie legal on these numbers may still be
cancelled by the convenor or declined by you, and neither decision needs the
other's agreement.

## Fuel

| State | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Committed to the approach | no diversion remains |
| Bingo | return now, whatever is incomplete |
| Emergency | declare it, land straight in |

Bingo is a number computed before each sortie for that day's aircraft and
destination. It is not a suggestion, and it does not move because you are one
circuit from finishing.

## Your own numbers

Two figures in this course are not issued by the school. You set them, you
defend them, and they are marked.

- your **decision altitude** — below which you stop attempting a recovery
- your **gates** — in the form *if not X by Y, then go around*

Both are on your card before week 10. Neither is checked against a school
answer, because there is not one.
