Most of what follows is about whether you are permitted to fly on a given day.
None of it is discretionary, and none of it is a judgement about you.

## Currency

Fourteen days. If more than fourteen days pass between your last sortie in the
box and your next one, you return to the simulator before the aircraft,
regardless of where you had got to.

Currency is not a punishment for absence. It is an observation about how
quickly the approach stops being automatic.

## Crew rest

Twelve hours between the end of any obligation and the start of a sortie
brief, of which eight are an opportunity to sleep. The schedule is built to
make this possible; you are responsible for using it.

No alcohol within twelve hours of a brief. This is a hard number, it is not
adjusted for how much, and the twelve hours runs to the **brief**, not to the
sortie.

If you have not slept, say so. A sortie stood down for crew rest costs the
school an aircraft slot. There is no other cost, and nothing is recorded
beyond the fact that the slot moved.

## Medical and grounding

You are grounded, by default, for: any illness affecting the ears, sinuses or
inner ear; any medication not cleared by the flight surgeon, including
over-the-counter medication; any blood donation within 72 hours; and any
condition you would hesitate to mention.

Self-grounding requires no permission and no explanation. Tell the crew chief
and the sortie does not happen.

The flight surgeon returns you to flying status, not the convenor. This is
deliberate: the person who wants the syllabus completed is not the person who
decides you are fit.

## Weather minima

Published on the [standards](/standards/) page, and they are minima rather
than targets. A sortie inside the minima may still be cancelled by the
convenor or declined by you. Neither decision requires the other's agreement.

You may always decline a sortie on weather. You do not have to be right.

## Missed sorties

A sortie missed for currency, crew rest, medical grounding or weather is
rescheduled with no academic consequence, and there is no limit on how many
times.

A sortie missed for any other reason follows the standard extension process
below.

## Academic integrity

The written judgement in week 11 is the only assessment in this course where
this is a live question, and the rule for it is specific: the week 10 account
it rests on must be the one you wrote the same day, unedited. Improving it
afterwards to make the argument tidier is the form of misconduct this course
is most likely to see, and the one it is most interested in.

Assistance of any kind is declared. Discussing your decision altitude with
other students is expected and is not assistance; having someone else produce
the arithmetic behind it is.

## Extensions

Arranged before the deadline, not after, and granted on request for the
written judgement. Flying assessments are not extended — they are
rescheduled, which is a different process and a more forgiving one.

## Getting help

Ask the person whose name is on the period. For anything that spans the
course, or anything you would rather not raise with the person marking you,
the convenor's contact is on the [people](/people/) page.
